Analog design · simulation

PWM signal generator

Sawtooth generator and comparator built from TL081 op-amps, designed against a fixed spec and validated in OrCAD PSpice.

  • 12 kHz output, duty cycle adjustable from 20% to 90%, amplitude 3–10 V
  • Transient, DC sweep and parametric analyses (timing capacitor vs. frequency, setpoint vs. duty cycle)
  • Monte Carlo analysis to check behavior under component tolerances
